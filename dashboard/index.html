OpenRails — Clearing & Settlement for Streamed Work

Intent-driven clearing and settlement infrastructure on Arc. In plain terms, a payment rail: sign an intent, clear it into a bounded Vault, settle value as work is performed, recover residual. Task-first control room for RailsFlow, RailsCard, Vault state, receipts, recovery, and x402 proof.
